Garage Buddy

Mileage, fuel, and running costs — for every car you own.

An iPhone app for people whose garage holds more than one set of keys. Snap the odometer to log it, watch what each car actually costs you, and get a nudge before service is due.

What it does

  • Log it in seconds. Photograph the odometer or the gas pump and Garage Buddy reads the numbers. On-device recognition is free, private, and works with no signal.
  • See the real cost. Miles per month by car, spend by category, cost per mile, and MPG per fill-up with a trend line — gas and electric on one chart.
  • Stay ahead of service. Reminders by date or by mileage, one-off or repeating, with a nudge the morning they are due.
  • Keep the paperwork. Scan receipts and documents to PDF, attach photos to any entry, and export a clean report for one car or the whole fleet.

Your data stays yours

Choose where everything lives when you first open the app:

  • On your iPhone — completely offline. No account, no sign-up, nothing to configure.
  • In your own Google Drive — an ordinary spreadsheet you can open and edit yourself, so your records are never locked inside an app.

You can switch between the two later and take everything with you.

There are no servers, no ads, no subscription, and no account. Nothing is collected about you, because there is nowhere for it to go. The privacy policy spells out exactly what the app touches.
