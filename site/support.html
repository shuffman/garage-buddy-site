Common questions

Do I need a Google account?

No. When you first open Garage Buddy you choose where your data lives. Pick “On this device” and the app works entirely offline with no account, no sign-up, and nothing to configure. Google Drive is an optional alternative, not a requirement.

What is the difference between the two storage modes?

On this device keeps everything on your iPhone, included in your normal device backup. Google Drive keeps your records in an ordinary spreadsheet in your own Drive, which you can open and edit yourself. You can switch between them at any time in Settings → Data, and your data moves with you in either direction.

Can Garage Buddy see the rest of my Google Drive?

No. The app requests only the drive.file permission, which grants access solely to the files it creates itself. The rest of your Drive is invisible to it. See the privacy policy for details.

Does the odometer scanning cost anything?

No. The default scanner uses Apple’s on-device text recognition — it is free, works with no signal, and never sends your photo anywhere. AI scanning is an optional extra for difficult photos, and it uses an API key you supply from a provider you choose. There is no subscription and no in-app purchase.

Why is my fuel economy missing or wrong?

MPG is calculated between fill-ups, so a car needs at least two gas entries with gallons recorded before a figure appears. Entries saved without an odometer reading are treated as “no reading” and are skipped by the mileage and economy calculations, which is why an entry can appear in your history without affecting the charts.

How do I get my data out?

Export a PDF report for a single car or the whole garage from the share button. In Google Drive mode your data is also always available as a normal spreadsheet you can download, and Drive keeps its own version history.

I deleted the app — is my data gone?

In Google Drive mode, no: your spreadsheet and folder remain in your Drive and are picked up again when you reinstall and sign in. In on-device mode, deleting the app deletes its data, so export a PDF or move your data to Google first if you want to keep it.

Reporting a bug

Please include what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and whether it repeats. Screenshots are ideal. If it involves a scan, the original photo helps more than anything else.
